# Audit and hardening, November 2026

What was reviewed, what was changed, and what is still open. Tests: `npm run test:db`, `test:loans`, `test:fines`, `test:fuzz`, `test:gsync`, `test:license` and the others still pass; the two loan-test checks about older drafts need `BONDHU_ADMIN_PASSWORD` to run (they fail the same way on the code before this audit when it is missing).

## 1. Entry and accounting audit (changed)
65 bad or odd inputs were fed to every money entry. 32 were accepted that should not be. Now refused or handled:
- Amounts: Infinity, text, absurdly large (above 10 billion), fractions of a paisa (rounded to 2 decimals).
- Dates: an impossible date (31/02/2025, "yesterday") used to be **silently replaced by today**; it is now refused. Future payment, expense, purchase and join dates are refused.
- Payments: a month before the member joined, more than a year ahead, or a payment date before joining is refused. A second payment for the same member and month **no longer silently replaces the first**: the app asks, and the old amount goes to the activity log.
- Expenses and investments larger than the cash in hand ask for confirmation ("the books will show negative cash") instead of going through quietly.
- Payment method, expense category and investment sector must come from the Master Data lists. Text fields have length limits.
- Members: monthly due above zero, opening balance not negative, valid join date, phone digits only.
- `2025-13` is no longer accepted as a month anywhere.
Books-balance stress test (hundreds of random operations) still passes after every step.

## 2. Loans (changed)
Review verdict before: maths and books sound (about 6/10 maturity: strong calculation, thin controls). Added:
- **Enforced lending policy** (Loan policy dialog): open loans per member (default 1), no new loan for an overdue or written-off borrower (default on), minimum membership months, most unpaid contribution months, one loan at most a % of the fund, cash reserve after a loan, guarantees per member (default 2). Only an **Admin** can approve an exception, with a written reason that is kept on the loan.
- **No silent deletion**: deleting a loan needs an Admin and a reason; removing a repayment needs a reason and is refused if it would make cash negative. The draft a deleted loan came from becomes a waiting draft again.
- **Portfolio quality** on the Loans screen: overdue by age (1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days), PAR30/60/90, and a defaulters list with guarantor.
Still open (not done, in priority order): approval workflow with rejected/withdrawn states and a second approver; printable repayment receipt, schedule and borrower statement; penalty engine (grace, rate, cap, waiver record); recovery after write-off and "call on guarantor"; reschedule / principal-only prepayment and per-day interest on early closure.

## 3. Performance (changed) and code
Measured on 500 members, 33,000 payments, 10 MB database: Overview 0.34 s to 0.25 s, Fines 0.69 s to 0.56 s, backup export 17 ms. Changes: a covering index on payments by date; no photo (logo/avatar) read on every call; one export per backup; date and month arithmetic without Date objects; savings by date by binary search; report preview shows the first 300 rows (totals and exports always use all rows); the licence screen no longer re-renders every minute.
Bugs found and fixed on the way: foreign keys switched off after any backup/sync export; login lock re-armed after it expired; money values cut off with "..." on narrow KPI cards.
Still open (measured or reviewed, not done): the 12-month trend recomputes the fund position 14 times (do one GROUP BY per table); reports fetch all rows to compute an opening balance; every write triggers a refresh of settings/users/notifications (narrow it); the arrears loop is copied in three places; `exceljs` is bundled eagerly (3 MB); top-bar search only switches to Members; the audit report reads only the latest 1000 entries before filtering; the member statement counts months after a member left; the Excel import counts ignored rows as added.
